Dr Qiang Zhu is a computational chemist and biophysicist dedicated to advancing molecular simulations for solving challenging problems in chemistry, biology, and medicine. His research combines theoretical modelling, high-performance computing, and artificial intelligence to understand how molecules assemble, interact, and function in complex environments.

His current research focuses on biomolecular condensates, protein aggregation, drug discovery, and multiscale simulation method development. By creating new computational tools and integrating physics-based and AI-driven approaches, he seeks to reveal molecular mechanisms that are difficult to access experimentally and to accelerate the translation of fundamental discoveries into biomedical and technological applications.

Dr Zhu earned his PhD from Nanjing University and completed postdoctoral training at both Nanjing University and the University of California, Irvine. He is currently a Vice-Chancellor's Research Fellow at the University of Wollongong, where he leads research on computational methods for biomolecular systems and therapeutic discovery.
