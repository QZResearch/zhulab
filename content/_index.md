Molecular simulation gives an exact picture of a system one atom at a time — and almost never at the scale a real material lives on. **Our group builds the bridge.** We derive coarse-grained representations from atomistic data, test how far they can be trusted, and use them to study self-assembly, transport and phase behaviour in soft and hybrid materials.

The work sits between statistical mechanics, applied mathematics and software engineering, and everything we build is released openly.
