I'm excited to be offering a project through the 2027 QUBIC Undergraduate Summer Internship Program, co-mentored by [Prof. Haibo Yu](https://scholars.uow.edu.au/haibo-yu).
 
This project will give an undergraduate student the opportunity to work at the frontier of quantum biotechnology research on exploring how molecular chirality influences amyloid fibril formation, gaining hands-on experience in molecular dynamics simulation, data analysis, and scientific visualisation, while contributing to research relevant to Alzheimer's disease.
 
The program runs for four weeks from January to February 2027 and is open to domestic undergraduate students currently in their second to final year of study.
 
If you're curious about research, looking to develop practical skills, and interested in gaining experience at the intersection of quantum technologies and biotechnology, we'd love to hear from you.
 
👉 Learn more about the project and apply: [https://lnkd.in/gSQG3UdH](https://lnkd.in/gSQG3UdH)

Applications close 16 October.
